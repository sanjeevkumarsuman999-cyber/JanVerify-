🔎 Why verification matters

JanVerify does not generate or alter the ration transaction. It compares an existing government/PDS record with the beneficiary's report. A mismatch becomes a case for review.

🔐 Record integrity

Government-record quantities are read-only in this prototype. Only the beneficiary-reported quantities and verification outcome can be submitted.
